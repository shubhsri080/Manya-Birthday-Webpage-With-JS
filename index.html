THE THINGS I ADMIRE

There are so many things I admire about you.

And honestly, I don't think I say these things nearly enough.

01

Your kindness

You're genuinely kind, and not just when it's convenient. You have this natural tendency to care about people and make them feel comfortable.

Recently, when I wasn't really having the best day, I ended up being unnecessarily cold with you. You could have taken it personally. Instead, you simply asked what was wrong, and when I said I was just in a bad mood, you didn't judge me.

You just said that everyone has days when their mood isn't right.

That small response meant more to me than you probably realised.

I genuinely felt really bad about the time when I didn't talk to you properly, I was rude to you just because I was having a bad day... not particularly a bad day but a bad and hurtful conversation with one of my friend. You had always been so nice to me, even when you were angry or going through lots and lots of mood swings. No matter what, you always knew how to keep your anger under control and never took it out on me. But I, on the other hand, became rude to you in just one moment, I showered someone else's anger on you, and honestly, I regretted it a lot.

Looking back, I realized that you had always treated me with patience and kindness, and I should have done the same for you. I'm really sorry for that, and I'm glad that despite all those little moments, our friendship still stayed strong.❤️

Because sometimes, you learn what a friendship means not from the huge moments, but from the small moments when someone could have walked away — and chooses to understand instead.

02

You're talented, but you still appreciate everyone else.

You're genuinely good at things — especially drawing. I still remember that wall-painting day when I was struggling to draw Snow White's face and you helped me with it.

But what I admire even more is that being good at something has never stopped you from appreciating other people's work.

You know your own worth without needing to make anyone else feel smaller.

03

Your strength

You're one of those people who can handle things without constantly needing everyone to know what you're going through.

There's a quiet strength in you. You know how to stand on your own, make your own decisions and keep moving forward.

04

Your independence

I really admire how independent you are. You don't need constant validation from everyone around you to know who you are.

You have your own personality, your own opinions and your own way of looking at things — and you aren't afraid to be yourself.

05

The softness you have

Maybe one of the simplest things I appreciate about you is how much you love animals, how kind you are towards them.

How you care about their well-being and their happiness.

It says something about a person when they can genuinely care about beings who can't give them anything in return.

06

The way you make people look at things differently

Thank you for being someone who supports me, motivates me, and reminds me not to give too much importance to things that simply aren't worth it.

And maybe that's what I appreciate most about our friendship.

We don't have years of photographs together. We don't even have some huge collection of memories from growing up together.

But somehow, we still ended up here. From two classmates who barely talked… to two people who genuinely enjoy having each other around.

And honestly, I'm really glad we did.

07

Your support

Thank you for always supporting me and helping me whenever I need you. You listen to me, make things feel a little easier, and I'm truly grateful to have you in my life. Thank you so much Manya, for being my unpaid therapist.
